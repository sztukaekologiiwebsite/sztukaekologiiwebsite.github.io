What we do

Small actions,real change.

Everything we do starts with listening to young people — then acting together, locally.

01

Local clean-ups

Six actions in the most polluted corners of Jedlicze and Krosno — for many volunteers, their first taste of real impact.

02

Reuse & circular economy

Free exchange markets for furniture, electronics and more — with music, games and over 70 items finding new homes.

03

Workshops in schools

Simulation games, group work and hands-on models that make environmental topics practical and engaging.

04

Youth wellbeing

A weekly support group: a safe, open, non-discriminatory space to talk about loneliness, pressure and climate anxiety.

05

International cooperation

Erasmus+ exchanges and partnerships that bring global perspectives back home to our community.
