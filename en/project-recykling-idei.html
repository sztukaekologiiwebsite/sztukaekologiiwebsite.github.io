Overview

About theproject.

Recykling Idei (Recycling Ideas) was created by young people aged 18–25 from Krosno and Jedlicze, with support from the Sztuka Ekologii Foundation. Funded under the Erasmus+ Youth Participation Activities programme, it runs from September 2026 to May 2027.

Over nine months, the group will lead workshops, community events and creative projects that connect ecology with mental wellbeing — combining art, ecology and non-formal education so young people can turn helplessness, anxiety and guilt into action, cooperation and a real sense of agency.

Why this project?

The idea came from the young people’s own experiences, backed by a survey they ran on World Sustainable Development Day in secondary schools in Krosno and at the Jedlicze Municipal Cultural Centre.

75%say towns like Krosno and Jedlicze don’t offer enough educational, cultural and personal development activities for young people
60%feel guilty throwing things away but don’t know how to reuse them
80%feel anxious and overwhelmed by the flood of information and the climate crisis
85%want to make new friends but face barriers like shyness and low self-confidence

The idea

What if the things we throw away could become instruments, clothes and art?

Chapter 01

Giving wastea second life.

Upcycling workshops led by artist Piotr Burger will teach students to design and build useful objects from wood, metal, plastic and textiles. The project’s young leaders will co-run the workshops as peer educators.

Before that, the group will see where waste goes: a visit to the Regional Waste Recovery Centre in Krosno and a meeting with Janusz Fic, president of the Krosno Municipal Holding, followed by a clean-up of green spaces in Krosno with local secondary school students.

Chapter 02

Swap,don’t shop.

Participants will learn about the environmental cost of fast fashion and design their own clothing collection made entirely from reclaimed materials.

A community clothing swap will invite local residents to give their things a new owner. Building on it, the group will create and pilot a free app that lets people in Krosno and the surrounding area exchange items they no longer need.

Chapter 03

Musicfrom rubbish.

Participants will build instruments from metal, plastic, wood and textiles, experiment with sound and write their own songs about consumption, wellbeing and responsibility for the planet.

Alongside, wellbeing workshops will cover stress, digital overload and screen habits, and paper sculpture sessions with Ewelina Jurasz, director of the Krosno Art Exhibition Bureau, will use recycled paper as a way to express emotions.

The plan

Nine months,nine steps.

Step 01 · September 2026

A creative start in Zakopane

A team trip focused on trust, wellbeing and ecology, including a visit to the Władysław Hasior Museum, whose art shows how discarded objects can take on new meaning.

Step 02

Seeing where waste goes

A visit to the Regional Waste Recovery Centre in Krosno, a meeting with the Krosno Municipal Holding, and a clean-up of green spaces with local students.

Step 03

Giving waste a second life

Upcycling workshops with artist Piotr Burger, co-run by the project’s young leaders as peer educators.

Step 04

Climate in practice

A “Measure Your Carbon Footprint” campaign with peer-led school workshops, a tree-planting action in Krosno and a youth debate: Can one person really make a difference for the climate?

Step 05

Circular fashion

Learning the cost of fast fashion and designing a clothing collection made entirely from reclaimed materials.

Step 06

Swap, don’t shop

A community clothing swap — and a free app for exchanging unwanted items in Krosno and the area.

Step 07

Wellbeing and mindfulness

Workshops on stress, digital overload and screen habits, plus paper sculpture sessions with recycled paper.

Step 08

Music from rubbish

Building instruments from waste and writing songs about consumption, wellbeing and the planet.

Step 09 · Spring 2027

The grand finale

A conference open to the whole community: an upcycled fashion show, a concert on instruments made from waste, the swap app presentation and a panel with young people, experts and local decision-makers.

European Youth Goals

A safe space at every stage

Learning that counts

Participants will document what they learn through Youthpass.

Built to last

The workshop materials, the swap app and the trees planted in Krosno are all meant to outlast the project.
